The show

Five strangers wake up on an empty island with nothing: no tools, no fire, no food. Every episode ends with a choice, and you vote in the comments on what they do next. The next episode shows what really happens, using real science and history. From the first fire to the first rocket.

Meet the cast

  • Bo: acts first, thinks later.
  • Mina: clever, loves facts.
  • Tank: huge, strong, gentle.
  • Pip: scared of everything.
  • Ora: has seen it all.
