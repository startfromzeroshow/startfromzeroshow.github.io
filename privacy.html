Last updated: 5 November 2026

Start From Zero Publisher is a private tool that uploads the creator's own videos to the creator's own TikTok account.

  1. Data we access: after the account owner approves it, the tool receives the TikTok account's basic profile information (display name, avatar, open ID) and the access tokens needed to upload videos.
  2. How it is used: only to upload the creator's videos and check their upload status.
  3. Storage: access tokens are stored only on the creator's own computer. Nothing is sent to any other server.
  4. Sharing: no data is sold, shared or given to anyone.
  5. Other people's data: the tool does not collect any data about viewers or other TikTok users.
  6. Deleting data: the owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok's settings (Security, Manage app permissions) and delete the local token file.
  7. This website: it has no cookies, no analytics and no forms.
